MEA
DOW

A design language built from one quiet idea: a single bloom on a flat field, held in place by type that is far too big for the page.

Display letters are architecture. Captions are footnotes. The subject sits between them, overlapping both, and is the only thing allowed to break the grid.

Calm Scale Layered

Bloom Design System v1.0 — palette, typography, spacing, components and composition rules for editorial posters, landing pages and social.

01

One saturated field, one warm neutral, one tiny hit of pollen. The blue does the work; cream carries every word; yellow appears only where the eye should land.

03

Layout & Space

12‑column grid, 8‑point spacing. Display words span the full width and are split across lines; captions occupy 2 columns at the far edges; the subject is centred slightly off‑axis.

Gutter 8px (mobile 8px, desktop 16px). Outer margin 16px mobile / 48px desktop. Square corners everywhere except pills.

06

Rules

Do
  • Let display type run off‑scale — 60–90% of the canvas width.
  • Split one word across two lines and offset the halves.
  • Overlap the subject on the type; let a fragment of type sit back over it at ~35% opacity.
  • Keep backgrounds a single flat hue.
  • Use yellow once per view.
Don't
  • Add more than one subject or image.
  • Use rounded corners, shadows or gradients on UI.
  • Justify caption blocks or centre them.
  • Set display type in anything but Bone or Blue.
  • Put small body text in Bone on Field Blue.
